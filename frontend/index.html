How FoodBridge works

Three simple steps from surplus to someone's plate.

1

Donors post surplus

Add what you have, how much, a photo, the pickup window and when it expires. It takes about a minute.

2

Receivers find and claim

Browse a live map, filter by distance and food type, and claim with one tap. Nobody else can take it.

3

Pick up and track impact

Email confirmations and reminders keep everyone on time, and every rescue adds to your impact numbers.
